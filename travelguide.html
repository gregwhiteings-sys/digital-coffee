🛣️ Major Routes & Toll Road Costs

Costs are one-way for light vehicles / standard cars. Return = ×2. Tariffs updated annually — verify before travel.

🚗 N3 — Johannesburg ↔ Durban

📍 Total route: Approx. 570km
🔢 Number of toll plazas: 4 (one-way)
💰 Estimated one-way total: ~R215 – R240
1. ✅ De Hoek (near Heidelberg / Gauteng–KZN border) ~R62
2. ✅ Wilge (near Harrismith) ~R58
3. ✅ Tugela (KZN midlands) ~R52
4. ✅ Mariannhill (Durban outer west) ~R45

🚗 N1 — Johannesburg ↔ Pretoria

📍 Total route: Approx. 60km
🔢 Number of toll plazas: 1 or 2 depending on route
💰 Estimated one-way total: ~R35 – R105
1. ✅ Buccleuch (Sandton / Midrand boundary) ~R36
2. ✅ Pumulani / Hammarskjold (Midrand / Centurion — optional route) ~R69

🚗 N2 — Durban ↔ Pietermaritzburg / South Coast

📍 Total route: Approx. 80–150km
🔢 Number of toll plazas: 1–2 depending on stretch
💰 Estimated one-way total: ~R35 – R85
1. ✅ Umgeni / Mvoti (North Coast / Ballito stretch) ~R36
2. ✅ Oribi (South Coast / Port Shepstone) ~R48

🚗 N4 — Johannesburg ↔ Mpumalanga / Komatipoort

📍 Total route: Approx. 350km
🔢 Number of toll plazas: 3 (one-way)
💰 Estimated one-way total: ~R160 – R185
1. ✅ N4 Gateway / Platinum (east of Pretoria) ~R50
2. ✅ Machado (near Mbombela / Nelspruit) ~R65
3. ✅ Komatipoort (near Mozambique border) ~R55

💡 Tip: Heavy vehicles, buses & motorcycles = different tariffs.
📱 Download e-toll / SANRAL app for live updates.
🚫 Many tolled routes have free alternative roads — just add more travel time.
