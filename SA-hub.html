🗺️ SA Offline Travel Guide — KZN & Gauteng

Costs are one-way for light vehicles / standard cars. Return = ×2. Always verify current tariffs before travel.

🚗 N3 — Johannesburg ↔ Durban

📍 Route: ~570km • 4 toll plazas • ~R215–R240 one-way
1. De Hoek ~R62
2. Wilge ~R58
3. Tugela ~R52
4. Mariannhill ~R45

🚗 N1 — Johannesburg ↔ Pretoria

📍 Route: ~60km • 1–2 plazas • ~R35–R105 one-way
1. Buccleuch ~R36
2. Pumulani (optional) ~R69

🚗 N2 — Durban ↔ South Coast

📍 Route: ~80–150km • 1–2 plazas • ~R35–R85 one-way
1. Umgeni / Mvoti ~R36
2. Oribi ~R48

🚗 N4 — Johannesburg ↔ Mpumalanga

📍 Route: ~350km • 3 plazas • ~R160–R185 one-way
1. N4 Gateway ~R50
2. Machado ~R65
3. Komatipoort ~R55
